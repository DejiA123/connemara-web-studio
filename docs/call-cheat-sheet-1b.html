At the visit your job is simple: let them see their own website, then ask for the yes.

At the visit (5 minutes)

1

Hand them your phone with their sample open. Let them scroll.

2

Open bookdirectconnemara.vercel.app, scroll to the calculator, and let them type their own price and nights.

3

Give the offer (on the right): €570, or 3 × €190, or €39 a month.

4

Ask: "Will I put you down as one of my first three?" Then stop talking.

If they say yes at the table

  • Say: "Great. I'll send you the invoice for the €285 deposit today."
  • Message Claude: "[Business] said yes, €570" (or €39 a month). You'll get the invoice wording and the contract.
  • Bank transfer is fine for the deposit.
  • Tell Claude, so the "places left" number on your website goes down by one.

If they say "let me think about it"

  • "Of course. The launch price is for my first three, until 20 December. Shall I give you a ring on [day]?"
  • Write CB + the day on your list. Leave the sample online so they can show it to family.

The offer (when they like the sample)

€570launch price (normally €950)
€285now, to start
€285when it's online
  • Or 3 payments of €190. Launch price for the first 3 clients, until 20 December.
  • "Too much up front?" Offer: nothing up front, €39 a month (12 months minimum, support included). After 15 payments the website is theirs. Push the €570 first: deposits are your December money.
  • Online by 1 March, or they get the deposit back.
  • No direct booking from the website by 30 June 2027? They pay nothing for monthly support until there is.
  • Payment: bank transfer is fine. Ask Claude for the invoice.

After a YES on the phone: message Claude

"[Business] said yes. Booking.com link: … Room prices: … OK to use their photos: yes/no. Visit on [day]."

Claude updates their sample before your visit.

Be warm, be patient, and let the sample do the selling.
