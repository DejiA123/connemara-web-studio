# Start here today

Things only you can do. About 2 hours in total.

## 1. Put your details in (5 min)
Edit `studio.json`: your name, landline/mobile, WhatsApp number (353…), email.
Commit and push: the site rebuilds itself in about a minute.

## 2. Stripe (20 min): so clients can pay deposits
1. Sign up at stripe.com as an individual / sole trader, add your bank account.
2. Payment Links → New → "Get Booked: founding deposit" €285. Copy the link.
3. Make two more: "Get Found" €210, and a €190 one for the 3-payment plan.
4. Paste them into `studio.json` → `stripe`. Push.

## 3. Revenue (20 min): before your first invoice
- ROS / myAccount → register for Income Tax as self-employed (form TR1 or via myAccount "Register for tax").
- Keep every receipt from today. Use `tools/invoice.html` for invoices.

## 4. Google Business Profile (15 min)
business.google.com → "Connemara Web Studio" → Service-area business (hide your home address) → areas: Connemara, Galway, Aran Islands → website: your GitHub Pages link.

## 5. WhatsApp Business (10 min)
Install, set the business name and description, add a catalogue with the 3 packages and your site link.

## 6. Business cards (10 min, ~€25)
Order 250 with a QR code to your site (Vistaprint or a local printer).

## 7. Tomorrow-ish: calls from 21 November
Open `docs/discovery-call.md` and the call script in your pack. Day 1: Spiddal, Bearna and Moycullen.
Five starter samples for Day 1 are already in `clients/` as drafts. Publish one only after the owner says yes.
