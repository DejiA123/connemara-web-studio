# Client content checklist (send after the deposit)

Copy this into a WhatsApp or email. Don't start building until you have it all.

**About you**
1. Business name exactly as you want it shown (Irish and English if both)
2. Two or three sentences about the house / business and you
3. Address and Eircode, plus any directions tips ("turn left at the church")
4. Phone, WhatsApp number (OK to show?), email for booking requests

**Rooms / services and prices**
5. Each room: name, bed type, ensuite?, sleeps how many, price per night (low / high season)
6. Minimum stay, check-in and check-out times
7. Deposit amount and cancellation policy
8. Your direct-booking discount (e.g. 10% below Booking.com)

**Photos** (phone photos are fine, landscape, in daylight)
9. Outside of the house, each room, breakfast, the view
10. Permission to reuse photos from your Booking.com / Airbnb listing? (yes/no)

**Calendars**
11. Booking.com: Extranet → Rates & Availability → Sync calendars → export link
12. Airbnb: Calendar → Availability → Connect calendars → export link

**Payments**
13. Do you have a Stripe account? If not, I'll walk you through it (10 minutes, your own bank account)

**Reviews**
14. 3 favourite guest reviews you'd like shown (copy them word for word)
