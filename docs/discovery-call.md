# Discovery call (30 min) and on-site demo

## Before the visit
- [ ] Sample built: `node new-sample.mjs "Name" --area "Town, Co. Galway" --phone "091 …"`, fill TODOs, set `"draft": false`, push
- [ ] Open the sample on your phone, Irish version too if Gaeltacht
- [ ] Their Booking.com page open in another tab (prices, room names)
- [ ] Contract printed or ready to send

## At the table (5 minutes)
1. Hand them your phone with their sample open. Say nothing for 20 seconds.
2. "Tap Book direct." Let them fill the dates; show the price estimate.
3. "This message comes straight to your WhatsApp: no commission."
4. Ask: "How many guests come back, or find you by word of mouth?"
   Multiply by their nightly rate × 15%: that's what they're giving away now.
5. Offer: "€570 instead of €950 for my first three. €285 to start, the rest when it's live, and you see it all before you pay the balance. If it hasn't brought you a direct booking by 30 June, the care plan is free until it does."
6. Close: "Will I put you down as one of the three?"
